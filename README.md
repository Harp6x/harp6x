### How I build

I don't write every line by hand — I architect, direct, and ship using AI as an engineering layer.

**My toolchain:** Claude Code, Codex, Windsurf (Cascade), Antigravity

I treat AI the way a film director treats a crew: I own the vision, the architecture, the product decisions. The AI handles implementation. Every codebase has CI, tests, type safety, and documentation — not because it's trendy, but because systems protect meaning.

### How I think

I'm drawn to mental models, psychology, and philosophy — not as academic exercises, but as operating tools.

- **Systems thinking** — I see connections before I see parts. Everything I build starts with architecture, not aesthetics.
- **First principles** — Most complexity is manufactured. Clarity is the real work.
- **Anti-fragility** — I design for resilience, not perfection. Rough edges that ship beat polished things that don't.
- **Enneagram 5w4** — The Investigator with an Individualist wing. I go deep, not wide. Capability is the core drive.
- **The examined life requires action, not just reflection.** Reflection without movement becomes self-indulgence.

I reject hustle culture, productivity-as-identity, and shallow branding. Depth over volume. Always.
